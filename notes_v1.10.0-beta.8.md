## Holly's Chess Analyzer v1.10.0-beta.8 (Pre-release)

### What's New
- **Report an Issue Tab**: Added a **Report an Issue** button to the top bar that opens [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Flopper1-1/chess-analyzer/issues) in your browser.
- **Post-Analysis Feedback Survey**: After analyzing a game and closing the app, a quick survey prompt offers three choices:
  - **Ok**: Opens the [User Feedback Survey](https://forms.gle/qeVYFdKAeZpdAWW97) and marks it completed.
  - **Ask me later**: Closes without suppressing future prompts.
  - **Do not ask again**: Permanently disables the survey prompt.
