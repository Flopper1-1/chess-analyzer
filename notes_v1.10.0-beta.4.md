## Holly's Chess Analyzer v1.10.0-beta.4 (Pre-release)

### What's New & Fixed
- **Audio Settings Tab**: Added a dedicated **Audio** tab in **Settings** with:
  - **Master Volume** slider (`0–100%`) applied across `.wav` and `.mp3` sound effects.
  - **Mute sounds when window is unfocused** toggle.
  - **Also mute Brilliant (!!) sounds when unfocused** sub-option (off by default, so Brilliant sounds still play even while unfocused).
- **Larger Annotation Template Library**: Increased the height of the template list in **Profile -> Annotations** so multiple templates are visible at once.
- **Stop Button Fixed**: Clicking **Stop** now immediately aborts the active Stockfish search subprocess instead of blocking the UI for up to 30 seconds.
