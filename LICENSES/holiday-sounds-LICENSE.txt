Holiday Sound Packs (Christmas, Halloween, New Year's)
Created by mixing Holly's Chess Analyzer base chess sound effects with
watermark-free open-source / CC0 sound effects and studio recordings from:
  - sgossner/VCSL (Versilian Community Sample Library, CC0-1.0: sleigh bells, glockenspiel, tubular bells, bell tree)
    https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  - sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 CE, CC0-1.0: sleigh bells, glockenspiel glisses, concert harp)
    https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  - wyzowl/sfx-kit (CC0-1.0: celebration fireworks, confetti, party popper)
    https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit
  - rse/soundfx (Freesound & SoundBible CC0 / CC-BY-3.0: fanfare, spooky laugh, resonance)
    https://github.com/rse/soundfx
  - lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds (100 CC0 SFX, Kenney RPG Audio & Music Jingles, CC0-1.0: Halloween creaks, gongs, thunder, pizzicato)
    https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds
  - Reodelbasalan/adbertiser-sfx (CC0-1.0: fireworks, pop_cork, glass_clink, applause_short)
    https://github.com/Reodelbasalan/adbertiser-sfx
