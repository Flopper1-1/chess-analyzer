What is Win Chance?

Centipawn evaluations (100 = 1 pawn) are mapped to an expected Win Percentage (0–100%) using the non-linear Lichess win-probability curve:

win% = 50 + 50 · (2 / (1 + e^(-0.00368208 · cp)) - 1)
  • Brilliant (!!): Sound piece sacrifice keeping win loss ≤ 2%
  • Great (!): Critical tactical find or sole winning move
  • Best (★): Top engine recommendation
  • Inaccuracy (?!): 5–10% win chance drop
  • Mistake (?): 10–20% win chance drop
  • Blunder (??): >20% win chance drop
  • Miss (✗): Missed a winning move (≥70% win%) dropping below 60%
