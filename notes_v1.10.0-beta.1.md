Pre-release `v1.10.0-beta.1` adds Profile & Theme Customizations:
- **App Icons** (`Fluent` default + custom square-cropped icons + OS-clock Halloween/Christmas/New Year holiday overrides, toggleable in Settings)
- **Sound Templates** (`Original (Default)`, `Legacy`, and editable custom templates with per-event and per-annotation sounds)
- **Board Templates** (`Brown & Tan`, `Black & White`, `Red & White`, `Blue & White`, custom hex/PNG boards, and holiday boards including non-reshuffling saved New Year palettes)
- **Backgrounds** (`Dark`, `Light`, `High Contrast`, `Aqua Blue`, and custom image backgrounds)
- **Pieces** (`Default` and custom per-piece PNG templates)
